```
Closes #173

Part 2/2 (part 1: #184). Supply sag for the power stage.

Sag model (exactly what it is): the push-pull stage draws a supply current i = |v|/L0 (normalised, at most 1;
class-B-like conduction). The rail is the state of a one-pole RC network: rail = 1 − d, with
d += (S·i − d)·α, α = attack (20 ms, reservoir discharge) while S·i > d, else release (200 ms, recharge through the
rectifier's source resistance). The ceiling is L = L0·rail. S = sagAmount, the fractional droop at full continuous
load, clamped to [0, 0.5], default 0. When L moves, the ADAA history is re-expressed to first order (dG/dγ = Φ), so
F(x[n−1]) is re-evaluated with the current ceiling and no second solve is needed.
Patent check: not a legal review; generic RC supply model. The research (#167, Q8) cites a patent on envelope-driven
sag from a search listing only; its claims were not read. sagAmount stays 0 until the owner reviews it.
Measured (S 0.3, master 10, 1 kHz 0 dBFS for 0.5 s at 192 kHz): droop 0.223, held peak −2.53 dB versus onset; droop
after 1 / 5 release times 0.083 / 0.0016 (e^-1 law); a second burst starts within 1 % of the first. Fuzz at
S 0.5 / 20 dB NFB at all 8 rates: finite and bounded (peak ≤ 1.78).
```
