## Failing tests / known breakage
- ASR (test_drive.cpp, "ASR within ADR-0005 D5"): 5 kHz @ 48 kHz is about -30 dB for every voicing (gate -40), and
  1 kHz at max drive is -52..-55 dB (gate -60). 44.1 kHz 5 kHz passes (-57..-73).
- Cause (my analysis, unverified): harmonics in the stage-0 decimator's transition band (fs/2..0.62 fs, D3 tw 0.06)
  fold to just below Nyquist with little rejection, e.g. the 5th harmonic of 5 kHz at 25 kHz folds to 23 kHz at 48k.
  Re-measure after the FPU switch: the SSE limit cycle Moth found in #96 may contribute at the -113 dB level only.
